Our Story

Founded on a simple idea: show up, and fix it right

PestGuard Pro was founded in 2009 by a second-generation exterminator who was tired of seeing customers re-billed for the same unresolved problem. We built our company around licensed technicians, transparent pricing, and treatment plans that target root causes — not quick sprays that wear off in a month.

Today we're a team of 40+ certified technicians serving residential and commercial properties across the region, with an in-house training program and a satisfaction guarantee on every service.
